Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

At RFY Interactive, accessible from our official website and mobile applications, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by RFY Interactive and how we use it.

Our games are designed to be fun and safe. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information (such as name, address, or phone number) to play.

However, our apps may use third-party services, such as Google AdMob, which automatically collect certain non-personal data for advertising purposes, such as device identifiers, IP addresses, and interaction data.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display ads in our free-to-play games. AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our apps or other apps on the internet. You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use our apps by visiting Google’s Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for general audiences and parties. We do not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our app, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
